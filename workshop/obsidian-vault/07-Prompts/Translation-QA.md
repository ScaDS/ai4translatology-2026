# Translation-QA

Attach [[Source-Text]], [[Project-Brief]], [[Wording-Table]],
[[Teaching-Corpus]] and the translation to review. Attach a selection from
[[Translation-Comparison]] rather than both comparison versions if possible.

```text
Review the attached German translation against Source-Text, Project-Brief
and Wording-Table. Check meaning, omissions, additions, terminology and
register. Return an issue table: source quote, target quote, applicable
rule, proposed issue, explanation, and suggested correction.
Use corpus segment IDs only when the segment supports your claim.
Do not rewrite the whole translation. Distinguish errors from optional
stylistic changes. Say explicitly when evidence is missing.
```

Accept or reject each finding in [[QA-Record]].
